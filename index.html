Propaganda sits on a separate axis: communication biased to advance a cause, regardless of accuracy.

Six manipulation techniques

🍒
Cherry-picking

Keep only the data or quotes that fit your story.

📈
Chart axis manipulation

Change the Y-axis so the trend looks bigger or smaller than it is.

🖼️
Image recontextualisation

Use a real photo from somewhere else entirely.

✂️
Quote mining

Crop a real statement so it means the opposite.

🎓
Manufactured authority

Put words in the mouth of someone credible.

⚖️
Loaded language

"Claims" vs "admits", "regime" vs "government". Words that bias the read.
